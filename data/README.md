# Data

`environments.csv` holds the aggregate figures from an exploratory survey of
eight founders, as published in section 03 of the essay ("The time-luck
inversion").

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `environment` | One of four environment types founders were asked about |
| `hours_per_month` | Self-reported hours per month spent in that environment, aggregated across the eight founders |
| `perceived_serendipity_out_of_5` | Rating of the serendipity that environment returns, on a 1 to 5 scale, aggregated across the eight founders |

These are founder-reported perceptions, not measured outcomes. With n = 8 the
figures are qualitative and exploratory, not statistically representative.
Individual responses are not published, to protect participants.
